# Supporting characters

People who appear in one story. Each of them has a look in
[`cast.yaml`](cast.yaml), so they're drawn the same way on every page.

---

## Mrs. Lindqvist

*Lost Signal*, Act 4 and the endings. Token `{LINDQVIST}`.

**Canon**
- Owns the farm field where Skylark lands, along with the fence and the
  sign. *(Lost Signal · act4.md)*
- Sharp when strangers climb her fence: people crossing it have left gates
  open, scared her cattle and broken things. She explains this "not loudly
  but very clearly." *(act4.md)*
- If you knock first, she listens to the whole story without interrupting,
  then puts down her coffee and picks up her truck keys. She drives you out,
  joins the search line and brings the pink flagging tape. *(act4.md)*
- *"If you'd knocked yesterday, I'd have driven you out there myself. My
  grandson would have loved it."* Said "not unkindly." *(endings.md)*
- *"All right. Go find your box."* *(endings.md)*

**Look:** sturdy farmer woman in her sixties, short silver hair, tan barn
jacket, rubber boots.

**What she's for:** asking permission. The same person helps or blocks you,
depending only on whether you knocked.

## Mr. Okafor

*Lost Signal*, Act 4 and the endings. Token `{OKAFOR}`.

**Canon**
- Lives across the road from the tall pine where Skylark is caught.
  Answers the door in a sweater with a cat on his shoulder. *(act4.md)*
- Listens to the whole story without interrupting. Has a pole saw. *(act4.md;
  structure.md)*
- *"In my day we got excited about a paper airplane."* *(act4.md)*
- *"Now tell me everything. From the beginning."* *(act4.md)*
- If Skylark is left in the tree, he keeps watch all winter and calls the
  school when a March storm brings it down. *(endings.md)*

**Look:** kind older man, close-cropped gray hair, glasses, cozy green
cardigan, an orange cat riding on his shoulder.

**What he's for:** the neighbour who's glad you asked. Asking for help is a
skill, and he's the reward for it.

## Officer Reyes

*Lost Signal*, Act 4. Token `{REYES}`.

**Canon**
- The park ranger at Clearwater Reservoir. You find her number on the sign
  by the boat ramp. *(act4.md)*
- Listens to the whole story, asks two very sensible questions, and then
  laughs. *"I've pulled a lot of things out of that reservoir. Never a
  weather balloon."* *(act4.md)*
- At nine on Sunday morning she meets you at the boat ramp and hands you
  Skylark, dripping, with a rubber glove. *(act4.md)*

**Look:** friendly park ranger woman, olive-green uniform, wide-brimmed
ranger hat, rubber gloves.

**What she's for:** "we call someone with a boat." When the water is too
cold and too deep, the right move is a phone call to a grown-up whose job
it is.
