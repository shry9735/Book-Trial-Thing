## Canon

What the books and the game already establish.

**Who he is**
- The maker space supervisor. Practical, safety-first, and genuinely
  enthusiastic about the kids' work even when he doesn't fully understand
  it. *(Book 2 · characters.md)*
- Not an Arduino person. He'll point out something that looks physically
  unsafe even when he couldn't say why it's wrong electrically. *(Book 2 ·
  characters.md)*
- Knows when to step in (soldering, anything unsafe) and when to step back.
- Drives the chase van and runs the helium tank in *Lost Signal*. Has
  "filled a lot of balloons for the county fair." Locks up the maker space
  at nine. *(Lost Signal · front.md, act1.md, act2.md)*
- **Stops anything unsafe, on the page, immediately.** Nobody wades into
  the reservoir. Nobody climbs the tree. No launch in the gusts. *(Lost
  Signal · structure.md, act4.md, endings.md)*
- In the game he's in the room at the fair, coffee in hand, and asks
  questions instead of handing over answers. *(game · story.js)*

**How he talks**
- Calm. Unhurried. Slightly dry. *(Book 2 · characters.md)*
- Asks "do you need a hand?" without hovering.
- After a crisis, one line that lands better than expected.
- When it's about safety, he doesn't ask. He says it once and it's
  settled.

## Proposed

*Everything below is new. None of it is in a book or the game yet. Keep,
change or cut; move what you keep up into Canon.*

### Before the maker space

Mr. Torres was a stage carpenter for twenty years: theatre sets, then
concert rigs. Everything he built had to go up fast, hold weight over
people's heads, and come down again the same night. That's where the
safety comes from. He has seen what a stressed cable does, and he can feel
a bad load path the way Spark can feel a loose wire.

It's also why he's not an Arduino person and doesn't mind saying so. He
knows a lot about one thing, and he respects people who know about another.

### Why he drives the van

Because he's the grown-up who says yes. Ms. Chen designed the project; Mr.
Torres is the one who says "I can drive, if somebody needs driving" at
seven at night. He thinks kids should be allowed to do real things, which
means somebody has to be there to stop the dangerous ones. He'd rather it
was him.

### What he wants, and what he's afraid of

- **Wants** them to do it themselves. "Your call. I just drive" is the
  most important thing he says.
- **Afraid of** exactly one thing: somebody getting hurt on his watch. It's
  the only time he raises his voice, and he doesn't need to raise it much.

### Habits

- **Coffee in hand,** always the same chipped mug that says *MEASURE TWICE*.
- **Reading glasses on a cord,** put on to look at something small and
  immediately pushed up onto his head, where he forgets them.
- **"Just saying."** His way of pointing at something without taking it
  over.
- **The drawer.** He knows exactly what's in every drawer of the maker
  space and will tell you which one, but he won't get it for you.
- **The hand on the shoulder,** which means stop. Every kid in the maker
  space knows it.

### People

- **Spark:** they both think with their hands. Torres won't let him solder
  alone, and Spark has stopped asking.
- **Bitsy:** she explains the code to him and he listens properly, which
  she likes more than she lets on.
- **D.U.D.E.A.D.:** was built at his bench, during his build week. Called
  him "kid" once. Torres let it go.
- **Ms. Chen:** her ally. She asks; he stops. They agree at the start of
  every project who handles what.
