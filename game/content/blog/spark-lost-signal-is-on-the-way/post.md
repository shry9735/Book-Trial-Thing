*SPARK! Lost Signal* is a choose-your-path book, and you're on the team.

You, Spark and Bitsy spent the fall building **Skylark**: a small foam box of electronics that rides a weather balloon to the edge of space and parachutes back down. Every few pages you make a choice, and the choice sends you to a different page.

## Choices that matter later

Some choices pay off right away. Others don't seem to matter at all until hours later, thirty kilometers up, or in a muddy field with the sun going down. That's how real engineering works: the decisions you make on the workbench are the ones that fly.

There are **fifteen endings**. Some are triumphs and some are disasters. Nobody gets hurt in any of them, but things can go very wrong, and when they do it's never just bad luck. Every ending happens because of something you chose.

## The team

- **Spark** built most of Skylark's hardware. If you can touch it, he understands it.
- **Bitsy** wrote all of Skylark's code, and reads a datasheet the way most people read a comic.
- **D.U.D.E.A.D.** isn't part of the mission, but he's coming anyway.

The book runs to 158 pages, with a Mission Log you keep in pencil as you go.
