Inside Ignite

One game for the student. One clear view for the grown-up.

It looks like a game, because it is one.

Students land in a workshop and work through short lessons: reading, video and hands-on builds. Lessons come in order, so nobody meets Ohm's Law before they've met a circuit, and the menu always says what unlocks the next one.

See the wrong answer, not just the grade.

A score of 39% tells a parent something went wrong. Ignite shows the exact question a student is stuck on, the answer they chose, and the one sentence that explains the right one. That's something you can help with at the kitchen table.

Measured against the standards schools use.

Pick a child and a grade, and see where their work lines up with the science, computer science and maths standards most US states build on. Our own plain-English wording, the official codes, and a clear note that the match is our reading, not an endorsement.
