Against the standards

{# ── The caveat, before any number ───────────────────────────────────── Deliberately above the results, not tucked in a footnote. A parent who reads "4 of 34" without knowing whose 34 it is has been misled. #}
There is no national curriculum in the United States. Each state sets its own, so what your child is actually held to depends on where you live. What is below are the widely used frameworks most state standards are built from — and our own reading of which of our lessons touch them. Nobody has audited that reading. Treat it as a conversation starter with a teacher, not a report card.
