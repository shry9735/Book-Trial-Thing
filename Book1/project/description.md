# Characters

## Spark - Hardware Hero
Spark loves circuits, sensors, and soldering. He explains how electricity flows and how to turn parts into working systems. He helps kids read schematics and build safely.

Gender: Male
Age: Young

## Bitsy • Code & AI Ace
Bitsy teaches logic, debugging, and data smarts—from blocks to Python, then computer vision and beginner AI projects. She shows how software brings hardware to life.

Gender: Female
Age: Young


# Target Audience

This book is targetting 10 year olds who are interested in learning computer science
